## What an AI Architect Actually Does in 2026

- The role is defined by the integration of LLMs within the product itself
    - **Classic Architect/Software Developer**: Uses AI tools (like Copilot) to help write code or implement products that do *not* use LLMs as a core feature.
    - **AI Architect**: Designs and implements solutions where LLMs are a fundamental component inside the product.

### Distinguishing AI Roles

- There are no strict boundaries between roles like AI Architect, AI Engineer, Software Architect, and Prompt Engineer
- **[The Architect's Role]** In a large company, the architect is the person responsible for:
    - Designing the architecture and the overall solution
    - Making key decisions regarding the technical direction
    - Determining what kind of architecture should be implemented

### AI Roles in Practice

- **[Role Distinctions]** While theoretically different, boundaries are often fluid:
    - **AI Engineer**: Focuses on implementing the architecture and solutions that have already been decided
    - **Prompt Engineer**: Focuses specifically on prompt quality and developing different approaches to improve prompt effectiveness
- **[Real-world Application]** In many cases, these roles are not isolated
    - A single person might act as both the AI Architect and the AI Engineer
    - Multiple roles often merge into one position

### The Scope of AI Architecture

- **[Model Selection vs. Architecture]** Choosing the right AI model is only one decision among many
    - The model itself is not the architecture
    - Architecture refers to designing the entire system surrounding the model
- **Core Architectural Responsibilities**
    - Context management
    - Reliability
    - Evaluation
    - Observability
    - Governance
    - Security

### Critical AI Architectural Decisions

- **[Evaluation]** This is one of the most important topics and a requirement for any reliable system
    - Because LLMs exhibit probabilistic behavior, evaluation is necessary to ensure consistency and quality
- **Core Technical Puzzles and Focus Areas**
    - Agents
    - Tools
    - CoT (Chain of Thought)
    - Context management
    - Structured output
    - Evaluations

### The Underestimated Skills of AI Architecture

- **[The Importance of Mastery]** While all technical areas are vital, an architect must master them all to build a reliable system
    - **Reliability**: Essential for consistent performance
    - **Structured Output**: Crucial for integrating LLM responses into programmatic workflows
- **[The Most Underestimated Skill]** Evaluation
    - Evaluation is identified as the most underestimated skill among experienced developers
    - Mastery of evaluation is a core requirement for an architect to ensure the overall reliability of the system
