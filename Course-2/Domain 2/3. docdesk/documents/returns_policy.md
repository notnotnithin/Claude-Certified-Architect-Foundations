# PyStack Mart — Returns Policy (Official)

Effective date: 2026-01-01
Policy version: 2.3

An order is eligible for return if the status is "Delivered" and the return is
requested within 30 days of delivery. The refund equals the full item price.
Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the item arrived damaged. Approved
refunds are processed within 5 business days.
