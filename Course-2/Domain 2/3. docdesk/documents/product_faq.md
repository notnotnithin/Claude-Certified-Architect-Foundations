# PyStack Mart — Product FAQ

## Shipping & Delivery
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days within India. Delivery is free for
Gold loyalty-tier customers.

## Returns & Refunds
Refunds are allowed only for orders with status "Delivered". Refunds must be
requested within 30 days of delivery.

## Warranty
Electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Accessories carry a 6-month
warranty.
