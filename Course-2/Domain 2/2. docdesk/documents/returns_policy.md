# PyStack Mart — Returns Policy (Official)

Effective date: 2026-01-01
Policy version: 2.3

## Eligibility
An order is eligible for return if the status is "Delivered" and the return is
requested within 30 days of delivery.

## Refund Amounts
The refund equals the full item price. Shipping charges are non-refundable
unless the item arrived damaged.

## Processing Time
Approved refunds are processed within 5 business days.
