# PyStack Mart — Returns Policy (Official)

Effective date: 2026-01-01
Policy version: 2.3

## Eligibility
An order is eligible for return if ALL of the following are true:
1. The order status is "Delivered".
2. The return is requested within 30 days of the delivery date.
3. The item is not listed in the Non-Returnable Items section below.

## Refund Amounts
The refund equals the full item price. Shipping charges are non-refundable
unless the item arrived damaged. For Gold-tier customers, shipping was free,
so no shipping refund applies.

## Non-Returnable Items
- Gift cards
- Downloadable software
- Perishable goods
- Personal care items that have been opened

## Processing Time
Approved refunds are processed within 5 business days. The amount reaches the
customer's original payment method within 7-10 business days after processing.

## Contact
For disputes, email support@pystackmart.example within the 30-day window.
