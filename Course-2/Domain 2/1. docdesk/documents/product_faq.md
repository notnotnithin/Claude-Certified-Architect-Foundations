# PyStack Mart — Product FAQ

## Shipping & Delivery
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days within India. Delivery is free for
Gold loyalty-tier customers. Orders marked "In Transit" are on the way and
cannot be cancelled.

## Returns & Refunds
Refunds are allowed only for orders with status "Delivered". Refunds must be
requested within 30 days of delivery. The full order amount is refunded to the
original payment method. Refunds of Rs. 500 or less are processed automatically;
larger refunds require human approval.

## Warranty
Electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Accessories (cables, sleeves,
stands) carry a 6-month warranty. Warranty does not cover physical damage.

## Loyalty Program
Customers earn 1 point per Rs. 100 spent. Silver tier starts at 500 points,
Gold tier at 2000 points. Gold members get free delivery and early access to
sales.
