Source: India EV Adoption Report 2026 (published 2026-03-10)

Electric vehicle sales in India crossed 2 million units in the 2025-26
financial year, a sharp rise from the previous year. Two-wheelers made up
the largest share of these sales, driven by lower running costs and rising
petrol prices. Government subsidies under the FAME scheme continued to
support buyers, though the per-vehicle subsidy was reduced compared to
earlier years. By March 2026, Pune had about 480 public charging points,
up from earlier in the year as new sites opened.
