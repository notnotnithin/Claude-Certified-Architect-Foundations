Source: Public Charging Note, Pune (published 2026-02-05)

As of early 2026, Pune had roughly 350 public charging points, mostly in
malls, IT parks, and fuel stations. Fast chargers can bring a car battery
to 80% in under an hour, while standard chargers take several hours. Home
charging remains the most common option for two-wheeler owners, using a
regular power socket overnight.
