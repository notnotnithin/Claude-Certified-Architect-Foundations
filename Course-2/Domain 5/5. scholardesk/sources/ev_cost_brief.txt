Source: EV Ownership Cost Brief (published 2026-01-20)

For a typical city commuter in Pune, an electric two-wheeler costs about
Rs 1.10 lakh on-road. Running costs are far lower than petrol: roughly
Rs 0.25 per km for electricity versus about Rs 2.50 per km for petrol.
Battery replacement remains the main long-term expense, though warranties
now commonly cover 3 to 5 years. Overall, most owners recover the higher
purchase price within 3 years of daily use.
