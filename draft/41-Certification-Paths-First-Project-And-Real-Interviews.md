## Certification Paths

- **[How to choose?]** Start by identifying the underlying motivation for the certification
    - If seeking employment: Ask the employer directly which specific certifications they require
    - If for personal growth: Associate certifications are a good starting point because they are broad

### Specific Certification Focuses

- **Associate**: A broad entry point for those starting out
- **Developer**: Focused on implementation-based work
- **Architect**: Focused on designing AI systems
- **AI Professional**: Aimed at advanced enterprise architecture processes

### Preparation Strategy for Architects

- **[Prioritize building]** Do not start with the exam; build something first
    - If you cannot build something at work, build a standalone AI system to gain practical experience

### Effective Certification Workflow

- **[The Sequence]** Follow a practical-to-theoretical progression
    - **1. Build**: Create a functional AI system (e.g., a Shop Assistant) to apply knowledge in practice
    - **2. Learn**: Use certification materials to expose gaps in knowledge and structure your understanding
    - **3. Certify**: Attempt the exam only after the first two stages are complete

### The Purpose of Certification Preparation

- It serves as a tool to:
    - Expose specific gaps in your technical knowledge
    - Provide a structured framework for your understanding of the subject

### Technical Interviewing for AI Roles

- **[The Importance of Experience]** Practical experience is the most critical component of a candidate's profile
    - Certifications alone do not prove deep understanding
- **Key Interview Questions to Gauge True Understanding**
    - "What AI system have you actually built?"
    - "What happened when the LLM behaved differently from what was expected?"
- **[Evaluating Problem-Solving]** Interviewers look for the reasoning behind the fix
    - Focus on how the person decided to resolve a specific problem
    - Understand the specific challenges they faced during implementation
- **Memorization vs. Understanding**
    - There is a significant difference between remembering questions (rote memorization) and truly understanding the underlying concepts

### Distinguishing Understanding from Memorization in Interviews

- **[The Challenge]** It is easy for candidates to memorize technical facts, but much harder to demonstrate genuine understanding
- **[Scenario-Based Testing]** Use questions based on specific scenarios to test depth
    - Present problems where several different answers are technically valid
    - Ask the candidate to determine and justify the "best" approach
    - **[Why this works]** This method prevents candidates from relying on rote memorization and forces them to demonstrate their decision-making reasoning
